Ethos & Provenance
“Furniture for public use should look beautiful and outlast the lease.”

A bar or a shop floor takes more punishment in a month than a home does in a year. We build commercial furniture with solid timber and multi-ply birch, joined properly, finished in hard-wearing surfaces and designed so that worn parts can be repaired rather than replaced.

Built to Take the Wear

Heat- and scratch-resistant tops, reinforced joints and commercial-grade hardware for all-day, every-day use.

One Team, Start to Finish

The people who design your pieces also build and install them, so one contact is accountable for the whole project.
