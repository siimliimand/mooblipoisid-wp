# Product Catalog Specification

## Purpose

Defines how the site presents Mööblipoisid's core products and in-house capabilities. Mööblipoisid makes custom furniture for commercial interiors — cafés, bars, restaurants and retail shops — and, occasionally, private-sector kitchens. The site must present these ranges clearly enough for a venue owner or shopfitter to recognise the offering and start an enquiry.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Hospitality Product Range

The site SHALL present the hospitality range (cafés, bars, restaurants), covering at minimum:

- Custom bar and service counters, often integrated with refrigeration, sinks and espresso stations
- Built-in banquette and booth seating
- Commercial-grade dining tables in scratch- and heat-resistant materials (compact laminate, sintered stone, treated hardwood)
- Barstools and chairs
- Waiter stations
- Acoustic wall paneling

#### Scenario: Café owner reviews the hospitality range

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the hospitality section
- **THEN** all listed hospitality product categories are visible in Estonian, Russian and English

### Requirement: Retail Product Range

The site SHALL present the retail range (shopfitting), covering at minimum:

- Point-of-sale (POS) checkout counters
- Modular display shelving
- Clothing racks
- Illuminated glass display cases
- Fitting room joinery
- Branded feature walls

#### Scenario: Retail brand reviews the shopfitting range

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the retail section
- **THEN** all listed retail product categories are visible in Estonian, Russian and English

### Requirement: Private Kitchen Range

The site SHALL present the private-sector kitchen offering: bespoke kitchen cabinetry, kitchen islands, pantries and integrated appliance housing, positioned as a secondary offer built with the same workshops as the commercial products.

#### Scenario: Homeowner enquires about a bespoke kitchen

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the private kitchens section
- **THEN** the offering is described together with a call to action leading to the enquiry form

### Requirement: In-House Capabilities

The site SHALL communicate that wood (CNC woodworking), metal and stone fabrication are all produced in-house, as this is a core differentiator: customers receive a complete matching package from one team.

#### Scenario: One-team message

- **WHEN** a visitor reads the capabilities section
- **THEN** the in-house wood, metal and stone production is described and tied to the product ranges

### Requirement: Project References

The site SHALL include a projects/references section showcasing completed work (venues, cafés, retail fit-outs) with photography, so visitors can judge quality before enquiring.

#### Scenario: Prospective client browses references

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the projects section
- **THEN** completed projects are presented with images and short descriptions
